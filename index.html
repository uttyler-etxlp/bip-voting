How it works

Simple. Fast. Your call.

1

Watch the pitch

Each student had exactly 60 seconds to answer three questions: What is the problem? What already exists? Why is their solution better?

2

Score it honestly

Rate each pitch on four plain-English questions using a 1–5 scale. Think like a real customer, not a judge. Your gut reaction is what matters.

3

Winners announced

Voting closes October 26. The top three scores win cash prizes. Winners are announced at a celebration event the week of November 3.

Who can vote? Any UT Tyler student, faculty, or staff with a university Google account. One vote per pitch per person. Sign in with your uttyler.edu or patriots.uttyler.edu email to participate.
